For a squat, the system includes both the body and the bar. Bar-only power is a separate measurement. This COM translation metric excludes motion and rotation of segments relative to the COM, muscle/tendon power and metabolic power.

Checks and limits
  • Seven analytic checks passed: static support, constant speed, accelerating mass energy, ballistic energy, noncoincident peaks, combined body/bar COM and threshold-duration interpolation.
  • Measured COM work differs from vertical COM mechanical-energy change by 3.2–5.3%. Peak power differs by 5.7–6.6% between velocity methods. The methods share data and assumptions; these are consistency checks, not an independently established accuracy bound.
  • COM motion is estimated from markers with generic segment mass fractions. The reported mass is 88 kg; quiet force implies about 91.8 kg. No mass or force amplitudes were silently rescaled.
  • The power baseline does not depend on a muscle-force sharing solution. Anatomical colours retain the existing conditional tension and joint-reaction estimates; their physiological accuracy is still under review.
  • A screened natural-speed squat force dataset lacks an accepted velocity reconstruction here. Unvalidated integrated power values are excluded.
  • Replays end before landing. An end hold separates them without slowing the movement. Neither repeated displays nor the held frame add physical work.
