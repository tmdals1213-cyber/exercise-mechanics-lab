Development reference

These are assumed standard-model tensions. The start is a prescribed jump-ready pose, not validated neutral standing. The jump motion still has internal transients; LAT1 mapping and missing scapular paths remain under development. Neutral shading means unmapped/review, not zero tension.

Mapping and calculation
  • The opaque skin is a display surface. Its region colors show muscle-group tension, not stress in the skin.
  • Group values sum modeled muscle-tendon element tensions on the selected side(s).
  • All views use one fixed absolute-N logarithmic color scale. The color ceiling is not a force/strength cap.
  • Surface regions are transferred from the anatomical atlas in the rest pose and simplified for display. Deep muscles do not have unique visible skin regions.
  • 83 samples are solved at the displayed motion times using a causal magnitude-and-continuity reference, with no strength ceiling.
  • The body and both displays stay geometrically fixed when force-reference parameters change.
