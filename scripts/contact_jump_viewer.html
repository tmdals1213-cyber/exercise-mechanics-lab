What the colors mean
  • Muscle colors show the sum of modeled element tensions in each muscle group, in N. This is not activation, stress or injury risk.
  • Visible articular bone surfaces use black-to-red for joint reaction force after accounting for muscle and external forces. They use a separate legend. These are surface projections of joint reaction, not cartilage or ligament stress.
  • The contour shows eight selected regions, averaged per side. Other modeled muscles remain colored on the body.
  • Unmapped surfaces and toe muscles are gray. Intrinsic-foot and plantar-fascial support remain unidentified.
Important model assumptions
  • Generic 180 cm, 90 kg rigid segments; standard anatomical and inertial parameters.
  • Ground reaction and COP are solved from floating-root dynamics, sticking contact, a 16-face inner circular Coulomb cone (mu=0.8) and declared movement/control goals. No floating-root motor.
  • Two medial scapular landmarks remain on a thorax-fixed ellipsoid. This is an approximation; its neutral geometry registration is not an anatomical validation.
  • Passive reference springs and a 20° shoulder reaction cone are assumptions. Magnitude and continuity select one force history; actual antagonist recruitment is not identified.
  • No personal strength ceiling, fatigue, neural activation model, ligament deformation, stress or tissue damage calculation.
