Calculation and scope
  • Measured marker motion is registered onto the existing rigid-segment skeleton by matching bone rotations within joint ranges. Generic segment dimensions and mass fractions remain assumptions. The estimated COM displacement from the source reference model is preserved and registered at the quiet pose. This is not an independent COM measurement or a new force validation. Foot registration errors are recorded separately.
  • Measured ground forces, center of pressure and free moment are applied at each foot. Force amplitudes are preserved. Floating-root mismatch is disclosed and is not applied as an additional support force.
  • 438 muscle elements and four scapula–thorax contact variables. Internal joint moments are balanced with nonnegative tension, passive reference laws, a 20° shoulder reaction cone and a magnitude/continuity selection criterion. No strength ceiling. This is one conditional solution; actual antagonist recruitment is not identified. A near-zero selected tension does not establish real muscle inactivity.
  • Muscle colors show scalar group tension sums. Joint surfaces use a separate black-to-red scale for joint cut reaction after muscle forces. Neither quantity is tissue stress or measured activation.
  • Intrinsic-foot muscles and plantar fascia remain unresolved; toe surfaces stay gray. The replay ends before landing. Three video replays are one measured repetition, not three separately solved repetitions.
High quadriceps estimate

The current model selects a high quadriceps peak. The native effective knee moment arm is about 2.3 cm, with opposing moments from selected biarticular muscles. Removing the temporal penalty changes the peak by less than 0.2%. These checks explain the numerical result without validating its physiological magnitude. Peak moment decomposition and sensitivity.
