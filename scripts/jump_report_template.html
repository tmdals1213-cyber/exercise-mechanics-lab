Connected-body jump diagnostics

Contact → takeoff → gravity-only flight → landing. The free root is balanced without a residual actuator. Native segment masses, inertia, oblique joint axes and coordinate couplers remain in the calculation.

Conditional tension solver implemented

Standard values, rigid segments and a rigid tendon are accepted project inputs. Draft GH-stable tension histories are available. LAT1 mapping, missing scapular paths and continuous jump motion remain development items.

What this does not establish

Numerical consistency does not validate a human jumping technique. Large internal motion transients remain. These poses and joint demands are not accepted estimates for an athlete, muscle recruitment, or tissue injury.

Current limits

  • Ground-force traces and landing duration are inputs. Peak landing force is not a predicted personal impact.
  • The long-jump calculation is incomplete; the failed recovery state is retained explicitly.
  • This vertical reference is not a validated Sargent reach test. Standing reach and maximum fingertip reach are not scored yet.
  • Scapular muscle integration, muscle-force sharing and independent length/width/PCSA controls remain separate development gates.

Next model gate

Regularize the motion across time and resolve long-jump recovery, then solve and inspect muscle–tendon forces. Do not infer muscle involvement from a surface color or net joint moment alone.
