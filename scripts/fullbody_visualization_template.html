Model and display
  • All colored anatomy and both surfaces use the same absolute-N logarithmic color scale.
  • Group values sum modeled muscle–tendon tensions. Whole-body rows may combine related groups; they are not activation percentages.
  • Foot intrinsics, trapezius, serratus, rhomboids and levator scapulae lack integrated force paths. Latissimus parameters remain under review.
  • Humanoid colors are an illustrative surface projection; skin stress is not calculated.
  • Reference parameters can change force sharing. No personal strength ceiling is imposed.
  • Repeats replay one reference motion; they do not simulate fatigue or new trials.
